Forests: The Lungs of Earth

Forests cover 31% of the planet’s surface and host diverse ecosystems

Types of Forests

Tropical

Hot, lush, biodiverse forests near the equator.

Temperature

Distinct seasons with deciduous & evergreen variety.

Boreal

Cold conifer forests crucial for carbon storage.

Uses of Forests

  • Provide timber & fuelwood
  • Prevent soil erosion
  • Source of food, medicine & shelter
  • Support recreation & tourism
  • Regulate water & climate systems
