Delicious Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp white vinegar

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degree Fahrenheat (175 degree celsius). Grease a 9-inch round pan.
  2. Sift flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Add water, oil, vanilla, and vinegar. Mix smooth.
  4. Pour batter into pan.
  5. Bake 30-35 mins until toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool 10 mins in pan, then transfer to rack to cool fully.
